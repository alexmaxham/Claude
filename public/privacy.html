Tasko Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Tasko is a to-do list app for the web and iPhone. This policy explains what it stores, where, and what you can do about it. In short: Tasko keeps only what it needs to run, never sells your data, and has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Without an account

You can use Tasko without signing in. Your tasks, categories, progress and settings are then stored only on your device (in your browser's storage, or in the iPhone app's storage) and never leave it.

With an account

If you sign in, Tasko stores the following so your data can sync across your devices:

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and profile picture they share. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email; Tasko then only sees an Apple relay address.
  • Your Tasko data: tasks (titles, descriptions, steps, due dates, categories), XP, levels, streaks, trophies and app settings.

This is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on Tasko's behalf under Google's Firebase privacy terms. Security rules allow only you to read or change your data.

Google Calendar

If you choose Sync now under Connected apps, Tasko asks Google for permission to manage calendar events. It uses that permission only to add, update and remove all-day events for your open, dated tasks, and it only ever changes events it created itself. It doesn't read your other events. The access token is kept in memory and is not stored. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tasko's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled on your device. No push server is involved and nothing about your tasks is sent anywhere to deliver it.

What Tasko doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone, other than the services named above that run the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, open your account (tap your profile picture → Account) and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all data saved to it. For Sign in with Apple accounts, it also revokes Tasko's access to your Apple ID. Events Tasko added to your Google Calendar stay there until you delete them. Data stored only on a device is removed when you sign out, clear the browser's site data, or delete the app.

Children

Tasko isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.
